date: 2026-04-07

Every few years I feel the urge to tear down my personal site and rebuild it from scratch. This is one of those times.

The previous version was a Jekyll blog that I'd been patching since around 2014. It worked, but adding anything new required wading through a decade of accumulated configuration and half-finished ideas. I finally decided the cost of maintenance outweighed the comfort of familiarity.

## Why Astro

I considered a few options:

- **Next.js** — powerful, but more than I need for a mostly-static blog with no server-side logic.
- **Hugo** — fast and simple, but Go templates feel alien after years of writing JavaScript.
- **Astro** — ships zero JavaScript by default, supports Markdown out of the box, and the component model is close enough to React that I could be productive immediately.

Astro won.

## What changed

- Posts are now Markdown files in `src/content/blog/`, rendered by Astro's content collections.
- No external comment system. If something resonates, you know where to find me.
- The design is intentionally minimal. I wanted to get out of my own way and focus on writing.

## What stayed the same

The old posts are still here. Some of them are embarrassingly basic, but they represent where I was at the time I wrote them, and removing them feels dishonest.

## Going forward

I'm aiming to write more consistently — not on a schedule, but whenever I've learned something worth sharing. Shorter posts, more often, rather than long essays that never get finished.

If you've been reading since the Jekyll days: thank you. If you're new: welcome.
